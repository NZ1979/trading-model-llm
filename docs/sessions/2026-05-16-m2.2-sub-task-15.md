### What shipped

Three new public functions in `data/replay/fill_simulator.py` plus a private refactor of #14's logic:

1. **`apply_bar_to_portfolio(*, bar_et, day_state, portfolio, config, resample_cache=None) -> BarApplicationResult`** — for one canonical 5-min bar timestamp, gathers each open position's bar (high, low, close) from its resampled 5-min frame, calls `portfolio.check_stops`, records any triggered stop-outs with `exit_reason="stop_hit"`, then calls `portfolio.mark_to_market` with the gathered close prices. Missing-bar tickers degrade visibly (omitted from price dicts; `check_stops` cannot false-positive; `mark_to_market` falls back to entry_price via `equity()`'s missing-key handling at `sim/portfolio.py:231-233`). Returns the triggered stop-outs as a tuple of `StopOut` rows and the emitted `EquityPoint` from MTM. Never raises on normal data shape.

2. **`flatten_at_eod(*, flatten_et, day_state, portfolio, config, resample_cache=None) -> EodFlattenResult`** — snapshots `portfolio.positions` before mutation (record_exit deletes from the dict), closes each at the bar's close with `exit_reason="eod_flatten"`. When the bar at `flatten_et` is missing for a ticker, falls back to the position's entry_price AND logs a WARNING (Rule 18 — visible degradation, not silent). Emits a final `mark_to_market` AFTER the flatten loop so the equity curve ends in a fully-cash state.

3. **`apply_day_to_portfolio(*, decisions, day_state, portfolio, config, decision_id_start=1) -> DayApplicationResult`** — the driver-facing orchestrator. For each of the 78 canonical 5-min ticks (09:30..15:55 ET):
   - Step 1: `apply_bar_to_portfolio(bar_et)` — stops fire first against this bar's high/low; then MTM is taken at this bar's close.
   - Step 2: every `TickDecision` with `tick_et == bar_et` is applied via the new private `_apply_one_decision` helper (in input order).
   
   After the per-bar loop, `flatten_at_eod` runs at the timeline's final timestamp (15:55 ET). Returns a `DayApplicationResult` aggregating fills, rejections, stop-outs, EOD exits, and the equity curve.

**Ordering rationale** (stops before decisions at the same tick): in live, the broker fills the stop async but reports it before the next 5-min eval. Same-tick re-entry after a stop is therefore possible: stop closes at apply_bar; decision at the same tick can open the opposite side. The transition table is unchanged from #14; the new wrinkle is just the temporal interleave.

### Two MTM points at 15:55 — intentional

When positions are open going into the final bar, the equity_curve has TWO points stamped 15:55:

1. The per-bar MTM from `apply_bar_to_portfolio(15:55)` — positions still open.
2. The post-flatten MTM from `flatten_at_eod(15:55)` — `n_open_positions=0`.

Both are kept because the report's drawdown timeline benefits from the full intraday path. Downstream consumers can dedup by timestamp if needed; documented in the `DayApplicationResult` docstring.

### Resample-cache plumbing

`apply_bar_to_portfolio`, `flatten_at_eod`, and `_apply_one_decision` all accept an optional `resample_cache: dict[str, pd.DataFrame] | None`. `apply_day_to_portfolio` allocates one cache per day and threads it through all three, so a ticker's 1-min → 5-min resample runs exactly once regardless of how many bars + decisions reference it during the day. Saves N-1 resample passes per ticker per day (with N≈78 bars, this matters).

### Notable design decisions surfaced and locked

- **Three new structured result rows** (`StopOut`, `EodExit`, plus the orchestration aggregate `DayApplicationResult`): mirrors #14's `RejectedEntry` philosophy — every closed position has a structured row the comparison report can group by reason without parsing logs.
- **`exit_reason="stop_hit"`** (matching the design doc string) populated on the closed Position; same convention as `exit_reason="flip"` from #14 and `exit_reason="eod_flatten"` from this sub-task. The `Position.exit_reason` field's docstring (sim/portfolio.py:58) explicitly enumerates these three values.
- **Stop fill price IS the stop price** (not `bar_low` for longs, not `bar_high` for shorts). Stops are market-on-trigger; no additional slippage. Matches `check_stops`' docstring (portfolio.py:289-294) and the design-doc § Simulation.
- **`flatten_at_eod` missing-bar fallback is `entry_price`** (not `cash` or NaN). A position closed at its own entry price has zero realized P&L for that position; the WARNING log makes the data gap visible without producing a false-negative trade outcome.
- **Decision IDs unchanged**: still advance per input `TickDecision` regardless of action (including Hold). `apply_day_to_portfolio` groups by `tick_et` for O(1) per-bar lookup but preserves chronological/input order within each tick.
- **`apply_decisions_to_portfolio` kept available** as a stable seam for ablation tests and the existing 31-test suite. It is no longer called from `run_replay` (the orchestrator is `apply_day_to_portfolio` now), but the function still works and is in the `__all__` export.
